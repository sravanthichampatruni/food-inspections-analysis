# Problem Statement

## Scenario
You are a data analyst for a city health department. Inspectors cannot visit every food business equally often, and leadership wants a data-driven way to prioritize inspections.

## Questions
1. What is the fail rate by facility type?
2. Which violations appear most often in failed inspections?
3. How has the fail rate changed over the years?
4. Which ZIP codes have the highest fail rates (at least 50 inspections)?
5. Which businesses failed 3 or more times?
6. Within each risk level, which facility types fail most?

## Deliverables
- Cleaned dataset and two notebooks (cleaning, EDA)
- SQL queries for each question
- Tableau dashboard with map and filters
- Findings and 3 recommendations
